Challenge

Public affairs consultancy demands rapid adaptation across entirely different policy environments, client agendas, and audiences — often simultaneously. Managing full strategic portfolios meant owning each client's communications cycle end-to-end: monitoring legislative developments, producing strategic analyses, drafting press materials, managing media relations, and advising on stakeholder engagement, all while priorities could shift overnight in response to a vote, a Commission announcement, or a media event.

Approach

I developed structured workflows to manage competing deadlines without losing strategic focus, and maintained close communication with both colleagues and clients to stay ahead of fast-moving political developments. Across sectors including mobility, transport, sustainability, technology, and EU public affairs, I translated complex regulatory developments into clear, audience-specific narratives. A project I am particularly proud of was a digital communications revamp for T4SM, a European organisation representing the taxi sector. Rather than reinforcing institutional messaging, I developed a campaign — The Ones Behind the Wheel — that shifted focus to the people the organisation represents. Through interviews and visual storytelling, it highlighted drivers' experiences to demonstrate T4SM's values of sustainability, innovation, and social responsibility in human terms.

Result

The T4SM campaign humanised the organisation's message, increased social media engagement, and generated reusable content across multiple communication channels. More broadly, the role sharpened my ability to work under genuine pressure while maintaining accuracy and quality — balancing simultaneous client portfolios across different sectors and policy environments within a professional Brussels consultancy.

Challenge

The campaign faced tight timelines, limited resources, and a competitive electoral environment. As Communications Director, I needed to manage the party's brand reputation, coordinate messaging across digital and offline channels, prepare the candidate for high-stakes debates, respond to media developments in real time, and oversee the production of all campaign materials while working alongside other senior campaign managers.

Approach

I developed an integrated communications strategy spanning digital media, outdoor advertising, debate preparation, and media relations. The approach centred on authentic storytelling of the candidate's background and values, rather than attacking opposition. I produced all audiovisual content in-house, managed the candidate's social media presence, coordinated with local and national media, briefed the candidate before public events, and maintained rapid response capability for news developments. Content strategy emphasised local connection and policy substance over personality-driven messaging.

Result

The campaign increased the party's vote count by 2,000 compared to the previous general election, grew the candidate's social media following by 500 and the local party branch by 200, and generated significant media coverage. The experience sharpened my ability to work at pace under pressure, manage cross-functional campaign teams, and maintain communication quality across multiple channels during a high-stakes political environment.
